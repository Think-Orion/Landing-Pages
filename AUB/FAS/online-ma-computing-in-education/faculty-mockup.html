Faculty section — three ways to carry the full bios

AUB's full bios run from 594 to 2,612 characters, a 4.4× range, and about 12,000 characters in total. No layout can show all of that inline without burying the page, so each option below keeps a scannable overview and reveals the full text on demand. All three are live — click them.

Five portraits are real; the rest show a placeholder until AUB sends them.

Option A

Keep the slider, open the bio in a dialog

The smallest change from what is built now. The row still scrolls four-up, and a click opens a centred panel with the portrait and full text. Trade-off: six of the ten are off-screen until you scroll, so most of the faculty are never seen by a visitor who does not drag the row.

Option B

Directory list, expanding in place — recommended

Every instructor is visible without scrolling sideways, each with a portrait, a role line and a two-line opening. Read full bio expands that row where it sits. All ten names and roles are in the page from the start, so the section is scannable and the text is indexable. Trade-off: it is the tallest option.

Dr. Hoda Baytiyeh Associate Dean for Online Education, Faculty of Arts and Sciences

Dr. Hoda Baytiyeh has B.E. in Computer Engineering from France and Ph.D. in Educational Technology from the University of Tennessee (Knoxville), USA. Upon graduation in 2009, she joined the Department of Education, in the Faculty of Arts and Sciences at the American University of Beirut. Her primary research areas of interest include the use of ICTs to sustain education during crises, online learning communities, women in engineering, impacts of social media on education and society, and disaster risk education and hazard mitigation focusing on community and schools’ engagement. Dr. Baytiyeh initiated two graduate online asynchronous programs that were launched in Fall 2022; the Diploma in Online Education and the MA in Computing in Education. She is currently Associate Dean for Online Education at the Faculty of Arts and Sciences. Dr. Baytiyeh represents AUB in the National committee of Digital Learning at the Lebanese Ministry of Education and Higher Education to set standards and criteria and authorize accreditation and recognition of online programs in Lebanon.

Dr. Hugo Hachem Researcher in adult education

Hugo-Henrik Hany Hachem is a Lebanese-Swedish researcher in adult education. He defended his PhD in 2023 at Örebro University, Sweden, where he researched the education of older adults. Then went through a postdoc at the intersection of AI, lifelong learning and higher education at Linköping University, Sweden. Hachem has published in international journals. He is also co-editor of Studies in the Education of Adults. Hachem is an active member of the European Society for Research on the Education of Adults and the ELOA (Education and Learning of Older Adults) network. Hachem's research interests include educational sociology, lifelong learning, qualitative research, educational gerontology, educational philosophy, and educational technology.

Dr. Mahmud Shihab is a senior educational leader and consultant with more than 30 years of experience in academic leadership, curriculum development, professional learning, digital transformation, and educational innovation. He has taught undergraduate and graduate courses in educational technology, designing and facilitating learning experiences across face-to-face, blended, and fully online environments. He also brings extensive teaching and leadership experience across the Lebanese, American, International Baccalaureate (IB), and French Baccalaureate educational programs. Dr. Shihab’s areas of expertise include K-12 academic leadership, educational technology, artificial intelligence in education, curriculum and instructional design, strategic planning, STEAM education, digital citizenship, and professional learning. An ISTE Certified Educator, Dr. Shihab received ISTE’s Making IT Happen Award for his leadership in advancing educational technology and served on the Stakeholder Advisory Council for the ISTE Standards for Students.

Amal BouZeineddine is currently a full-time faculty member in the Department of Education at the American University of Beirut (AUB) and is the Interim Director for the Center for Teaching and Learning at the AUB. Dr. BouZeineddine has earned her Ed. D. in Bilingual Education from Boston University in Massachusetts, USA. Prof. Haidar Safa Professor, Department of Computer Science

Prof. Haidar Safa earned a B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science in 1991 from the Lebanese University in Beirut, an M.Sc. in Computer Science in 1996 from the University of Quebec at Montreal (UQAM), and a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering in 2001 from École Polytechnique de Montréal, Quebec, Canada. In 2003, he moved to the American University of Beirut (AUB) as an assistant professor and now holds the rank of professor in the Department of Computer Science, which he chaired for six years. During his time at AUB, Prof. Safa has built a strong record of research, funding, and graduate supervision, authoring more than 100 papers published largely in high-impact journals, magazines, and refereed conferences. His research centers on networking and security, with a growing focus on machine learning applications.
