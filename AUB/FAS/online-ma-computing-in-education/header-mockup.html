Cold audience header — closer to the EMBA strip

The strip colour, the reversed lockup and the hero-text-over-image structure already match the Executive MBA site. The one thing EMBA has that we don't is a row of section links. That is what makes their header read as a site header rather than a landing-page bar. Three ways to close the gap, each shown over the real cold hero.

Six links fit, and so would seven. Measured by stepping the viewport in 20px increments with the pill in place: six labels hold a single row down to ~920px, seven down to ~1020px, both with clear space either side. Width is not the constraint at desktop sizes — the constraint is the tablet collapse below, which applies at any label count.

Below roughly 960px the link row has to collapse in every option — that is where the labels stop fitting beside the lockup, and it is well above tablet width, so it is not an edge case. It would go behind a hamburger, or simply be dropped, leaving the lockup and the CTA, which is exactly what ships today. EMBA does the same.

One build note: only two sections on the cold page currently have IDs (#how-it-works and #form). Wiring a link row means adding anchors to the other four — a small change, but the links are inert until it happens.
