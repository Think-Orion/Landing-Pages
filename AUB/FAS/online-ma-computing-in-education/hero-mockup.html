Contrast is not a concern in any of them. Measured rather than assumed: with the type hidden, the lightest pixel anywhere under the headline is rgb(79,73,77), which puts white at 8.78:1 — above the 7:1 AAA line, and that is the worst point, not the average. The black scrim at 64% was already doing the work; the burgundy block was never load-bearing for legibility, only for emphasis.
