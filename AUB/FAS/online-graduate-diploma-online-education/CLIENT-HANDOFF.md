## How to read this checklist

The work happens in two stages, and the order matters:

1. **Before the pages go live** — content and decisions we still need from you (Sections A–C).
2. **Once the pages are on a real web address** — checks that are impossible to do before
   then (Section D). This is not us deferring work: a browser tab icon, a tracking tag and a
   form submission genuinely cannot be tested on a file sitting in a folder.

We recommend putting the pages on a **test or staging address first**, working through
Section D there, and only then pointing your advertising at them.

## Section C — Connecting the leads

**This is the most important section.** Today the forms are visual mock-ups. They look
complete, but **nothing is submitted and no lead is captured.** If the pages went live as
they are, you would run advertising and receive nothing.

- [ ] **Connect the real form** so submissions reach your CRM. *Your web team and Think Orion
      handle this together; the pages already have the connection points prepared.*
- [ ] **Check every field has somewhere to go.** The form asks for first name, last name,
      email, phone, **preferred contact method**, and **whether the person holds a bachelor's
      degree**. Those last two are the ones that usually get lost — if your CRM has no field
      for them, that answer disappears silently. The bachelor's answer is your fastest
      eligibility filter, so it is worth keeping.
- [ ] **Decide who receives each lead** and how quickly. The page promises a call within
      48 hours, so the lead needs to reach a named person, not a shared inbox nobody owns.
- [ ] **Keep the advertising source attached to the lead.** When someone arrives from a Google
      or Meta ad, the campaign information travels in the web address. If it is not passed
      into the CRM with the lead, you will know how many enquiries you received but not which
      campaign produced them. Ask your web team to confirm this is preserved.
- [ ] **Agree what happens to duplicates and junk.** Paid pages attract automated spam. Decide
      whether the CRM merges repeat enquiries, and whether the form needs spam protection.

## Section D — Checks that can only be done once the pages are live

Work through this list on the live or staging web address. Several of these **cannot** be
checked any earlier, which is why they are separated out.

### The essential one

- [ ] **Submit a real test enquiry yourself.** Use your own name and phone number, then
      confirm all of the following:
      - the thank-you page appears after you press submit
      - the enquiry arrives in the CRM, with every field filled in
      - the assigned advisor is notified
      - any automatic confirmation email reaches you
      - the enquiry is attributed to the right source

  *Do this before any advertising spend starts. It is the single check that catches the most
  expensive kind of failure, and it takes five minutes.*

### Tracking

- [ ] **Confirm the tracking tag fires on all three pages.** AUB's tag manager code is already
      installed on each page exactly as your operations team supplied it.
- [ ] **Confirm the thank-you page registers a conversion.** This matters more than the other
      two: a visit to the thank-you page is normally what counts as an enquiry in your
      advertising reports. If that one tag is wrong, your campaigns will look like they are
      producing nothing while still spending.
- [ ] **Confirm the cookie and consent setup** is in place if you advertise to people in
      Europe. *This lives in your tag manager, not in the pages themselves — one for your
      operations team.*

### Appearance and links

- [ ] **Check the AUB logo appears in the browser tab.** This depends on the icon files being
      placed correctly by your web team, and it will look broken until they are.
- [ ] **Post a page link into LinkedIn or WhatsApp** and check the preview shows the right
      title, description and image. *The image is supplied; this confirms your web team
      uploaded it and pointed the page at it.*
- [ ] **Open all three pages on a real phone**, not just a desktop browser. The majority of
      your traffic will be mobile.
- [ ] **Click every button and link**, including the three footer policy links, the factsheet
      download and the booking button.
- [ ] **Download the factsheet from the live page** and confirm it opens correctly.

### Search

- [ ] **Confirm all three pages are hidden from search engines.** Per your decision, none of
      these pages should be listed in Google, so all three now instruct search engines not to
      index them. This can only be verified once they are live.
- [ ] **Confirm each page's official web address is set** once you know the final addresses.
      *Technical detail in `DEPLOY.md`; your web team will recognise it.*

## Section E — Please do not change these

If something here looks like a mistake, it is deliberate. Ask us before editing.

- **The tracking code.** Your operations team supplied it with the instruction "do not modify
  this code". It looks unusual because it deliberately loads from an AUB web address rather
  than Google's, so ad-blockers do not interfere with it. Reformatting it breaks it.
- **All three pages being hidden from search engines.** This is your decision, applied
  deliberately. For the thank-you page it also stops your enquiry numbers being inflated by
  people who never filled in the form. One consequence to be aware of: because the pages are
  not indexed, the two landing pages now share the same set of frequently asked questions. If
  anyone later decides these pages *should* appear in Google, tell us first — the two FAQ sets
  have to be made different again, or the pages will compete with each other.
- **The thank-you page having no sharing image or preview.** Also intentional. It is a private
  confirmation screen, not something to be shared.
- **The factsheet file we supplied.** It is the compressed version. The original 22MB file is
  slow to download on a phone; this one is visually identical at 1.8MB.
