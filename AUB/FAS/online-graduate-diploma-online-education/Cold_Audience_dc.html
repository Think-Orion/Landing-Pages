Our Graduate Diploma in Online Education is an entry point into educational technology. It is made for educators who do not have a coding or IT background. The curriculum is built on instructional design: how to plan, build, and run learning that works online.

Technical skills grow alongside the teaching skills. You do not need them before you start.

The diploma is 12 credits across 4 courses, over 2 semesters. You graduate with two credentials: an AUB diploma recognized by NYSED, plus the Microsoft Certified Educator (MCE) certificate.

Many of our students have taught for years. They know digital learning is now part of the job. The diploma gives them a structured place to build those skills.

The Curriculum

What you learn in the four courses.

The diploma is 12 credits: 4 courses of 3 credits each. Each course takes about seven weeks, and most students finish in 9 months.

01
EDUC 371 · Digital Citizenship Learn the ethics of technology: responsible ways to access, manage, exchange, and share information online. Taught by Dr. Hugo Hachem.
02
EDUC 372 · Foundations in Distance Education Learn the fundamentals of online learning, for K-12 students and adult learners, in formal and informal settings. Taught by Dr. Mahmud Shihab.
03
EDUC 373 · Instructional Design and Development Learn the core principles, tools, and techniques of instructional design, then apply them in real course projects. Taught by Rayan Fayed and Rana Ghazzi.
04
EDUC 374 · Technology and Information Systems Explore how schools use technology and information systems: for decision-making, planning, and communication. Taught by Dr. Mahmud Shihab.

Your Journey

What changes for you, step by step.

These five stages follow your nine months in order: the four courses, then the MCE certification, then graduation with two credentials.

Early in the program

You understand how online learning works.

The first courses cover digital citizenship and the foundations of distance education. You get a clear base for everything that follows.

A few months in

You design your first online lessons.

Instructional design principles turn your ideas into real courses. You build learning materials you can use in your own work.

Midway through

You work with school technology systems.

You learn how schools use technology and information systems: for decisions, planning, and communication.

The final stretch

You complete the MCE certification.

The Microsoft Certified Educator work is built into the program. You finish the assessment alongside your coursework.

After graduating

You hold two credentials and a built module.

An AUB diploma recognized by NYSED, the MCE certificate, and a course design plan for 4 modules with 1 module fully built on the platform. If you want more, all 12 credits transfer into our MA in Computing in Education.

To apply for the Graduate Diploma in Online Education at AUB, you need a bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, an English proficiency test, and two recommendation letters. Graduates of all backgrounds and majors can apply. Work experience helps but is not required.

Yes. The AUB diploma is 100% online and asynchronous, with no fixed class times, so it fits around a full-time job. Each of the 4 courses runs about seven weeks, and most weeks involve readings, a short task, and a piece of design work. You choose when the work happens.

The Microsoft Certified Educator (MCE) preparation is built into the AUB diploma, and you earn the MCE certificate by the end of the program, at no extra cost.

The Graduate Diploma in Online Education is awarded by the American University of Beirut (AUB) and recognized by the New York State Education Department (NYSED), which gives it standing in the US and with international employers. One note for Lebanon-based applicants: the program is not currently accredited by the Lebanese Ministry of Education. An AUB advisor can explain what this means for your situation.

All 12 diploma credits transfer directly into AUB’s MA in Computing in Education. Some of our graduates choose to continue, and the diploma is also a complete credential on its own. You decide at the end of the diploma, not before you start.

Tuition for the AUB Graduate Diploma in Online Education is $450 per credit, or $5,400 in total for the 12-credit program. AUB alumni and staff get 15% off, and payment plans are available through the Comptroller’s office. If your employer sponsors staff study, AUB’s admissions team can provide the documents you need.
