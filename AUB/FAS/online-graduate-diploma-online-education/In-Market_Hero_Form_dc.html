The Credential Gap

Your school is going digital. Are your qualifications keeping up?

Your school expects you to run online classes, design blended curricula, and integrate tools you were never trained on. The gap between what your role demands and what your qualifications say is growing, and colleagues who close it first get the leadership roles, the promotions, and the mandate to shape how their institutions teach.

Our Graduate Diploma in Online Education gives you both the skills and the proof. In nine months you learn to design engaging courses, lead digital classrooms, and implement EdTech at an institutional level. You finish with an AUB diploma recognized by NYSED and the MCE certificate from Microsoft, two credentials that work on your CV the day you graduate.

Curriculum

What you learn in the four courses.

Four courses over nine months. Each one is practical and project-based, designed to give you skills you can apply the same week.

Learn the ethics of technology: responsible ways to access, manage, exchange, and share information online. Taught by Dr. Hoda Baytiyeh.

Learn the fundamentals of online learning, for K-12 students and adult learners, in formal and informal settings. Taught by Dr. Hugo Hachem.

Learn the core principles, tools, and techniques of instructional design, then apply them in real course projects. Taught by Rayane Fayed and Rana al Ghazzi.

Explore how schools use technology and information systems at the administrative level: for decision-making, planning, and communication. Taught by Dr. Mahmud Shihab.

Common Questions

Your questions, answered.

No. The AUB Graduate Diploma in Online Education is an entry point into educational technology, built for educators without a coding or IT background. Technical skills are taught step by step, alongside the teaching skills, so you do not need them before you start.

To apply for the Graduate Diploma in Online Education at AUB, you need a bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, an English proficiency test, and two recommendation letters. Graduates of all backgrounds and majors can apply. Work experience helps but is not required.

Yes. The AUB diploma is 100% online and asynchronous, with no fixed class times, so it fits around a full-time job. Each of the 4 courses runs about seven weeks, and most weeks involve readings, a short task, and a piece of design work. You choose when the work happens.

The Microsoft Certified Educator (MCE) preparation is built into the AUB diploma, and you earn the MCE certificate by the end of the program, at no extra cost.

The Graduate Diploma in Online Education is awarded by the American University of Beirut (AUB) and recognized by the New York State Education Department (NYSED), which gives it standing in the US and with international employers. One note for Lebanon-based applicants: the program is not currently accredited by the Lebanese Ministry of Education. An AUB advisor can explain what this means for your situation.

All 12 diploma credits transfer directly into AUB’s MA in Computing in Education. Some of our graduates choose to continue, and the diploma is also a complete credential on its own. You decide at the end of the diploma, not before you start.

Tuition for the AUB Graduate Diploma in Online Education is $450 per credit, or $5,400 in total for the 12-credit program. AUB alumni and staff get 15% off, and payment plans are available through the Comptroller’s office. If your employer sponsors staff study, AUB’s admissions team can provide the documents you need.
