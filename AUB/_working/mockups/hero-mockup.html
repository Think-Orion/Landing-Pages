Rather than eyeball it, I measured the two EMBA screenshots directly — pixel analysis of the burgundy button, the white text bands and the strip gutter — then solved for the Roboto settings that reproduce those widths and cap heights. Both screenshots agree to within about 4%, so the numbers below are stable rather than one-off.

Two places I deliberately stopped short of their numbers, and why. Their headline-to-body ratio is 3.68; matching it would put ours at 66px. But their headline is two short lines and ours is four long ones, so the same ratio reads as a wall of type. 64px keeps the relationship close without that. And their strip is 102px at a 1892px-wide capture — proportionally about 97px for us. Theirs is a brochure site; ours is sticky on a lead-gen page, where a 97px bar eats real estate on every scroll. 86px closes most of the gap and stays usable.

One honest limit: I have their site at a single viewport width, so I cannot see their clamp curve. If EMBA's type is already at its maximum at 1892px, these values are right. If it is still growing, theirs would be smaller than measured at our 1440 target. Send a narrower screenshot of their page and I can pin it exactly.
